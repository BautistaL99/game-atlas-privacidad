Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved? Personal progress and favorites are stored locally. You can export a backup from My Game before changing devices.

Is the community map official? No. Community locations and content are unofficial, and some data may be provisional until independently verified.

Are purchases available? Purchases and advertising have not yet been activated in this release.
