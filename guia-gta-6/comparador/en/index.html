How the comparison tool works

In Game Atlas, browse vehicles, weapons, missions, properties and places. Eligible catalog cards contain a Compare button, so you can select up to three items in one category. A compact bar tracks your selection and enables the comparison action once two or more items are selected.

You can also open the comparison tool from the navigation menu or an individual item page. Verified available facts appear first; fields without confirmed values stay under an expandable section called Show pending technical fields.

What can you compare?

  • Vehicles: top speed, acceleration, weight, seat count and other attributes when supported by verified sources.
  • Browse weapons →
  • Missions: duration and reward; when both are verified for at least two missions, Game Atlas computes reward per minute and clearly identifies it as a derived metric, not an official Rockstar statistic.
  • Properties, businesses and places: type, services, revenue or location details whenever they have been confirmed for each item.

How colors and unknown values work

Green and red only highlight numerical extremes with compatible units and an explicitly defined comparison direction. They do not necessarily identify the best overall item. A dash (—) means the value is unknown, not zero.

Potential field names inspired by GTA V do not establish that a mechanic or statistic exists in GTA VI. Catalog values require review and verification; no numbers are invented to fill gaps.

Selections and personal data

Comparison selections are temporary page state. They do not change favorites, personal progress or catalog records. You do not need to share your game progress to compare items.
